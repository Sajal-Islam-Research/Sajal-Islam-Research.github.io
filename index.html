Research

Making wide-bandgap power electronics survive the radiation of space.

Power electronics for satellites, lunar systems and deep-space missions need devices that block thousands of volts efficiently. Wide-bandgap semiconductors do that far better than silicon, but a single heavy ion can make them leak or burn out at a fraction of their rated voltage.

My research aims to improve the radiation hardness of these devices. I combine ion-strike simulation, electrical and materials characterization and controlled irradiation experiments to find where and why devices fail, and use that to propose radiation-robust designs. I did my Ph.D. with Prof. Ronald D. Schrimpf at Vanderbilt's Institute for Space and Defense Electronics, and continue this work at IU-CREATE.

Three materials

Silicon carbide (4H)~3.3 eV
SiC

Burnout and leakage in diodes and MOSFETs rated 1.2 kV to 10 kV, and physical models that predict their thresholds.

Gallium nitride~3.4 eV
GaN

Vertical PiN diodes and AlGaN/GaN transistors: how ion LET, epitaxial thickness and edge termination set failure.

Beta gallium oxide~4.8 eV
β-Ga₂O₃

Schottky barrier diodes with Pt/PtOx contacts and high-k field plates that raise the burnout threshold.

Funded projects

Air Force Center of Excellence

Raising burnout tolerance in power devices

Irradiation studies of β-Ga₂O₃ Schottky diodes, SiC diodes and SiC MOSFETs with heavy ions, Cf-252 and Am-241, locating damage to guide mitigation.

NASA LuSTR

Radiation-hard SiC for the lunar surface

Ion-strike simulation of stopping range and energy deposition, and experiments on high-voltage SiC devices, including at lunar-night temperatures.

NASA SBIR

GaN power devices under radiation

Experiments and defect characterization on vertical GaN PiN diodes to improve their performance in radiation environments.

Data for AI-driven reliability

Through Vanderbilt's VALIANT initiative, I generate experimental and modeling datasets that train and validate AI models, which predict device performance, degradation and failure and guide device design.

Irradiation tests→Failure datasets→AI models→Hardened designs

Before radiation effects: M.S. research

At Missouri State University (2019–2021), with Prof. Kartik Ghosh, I worked on thin films and heterostructures for energy and magnetic applications. My main project was a low-cost FTO/TiO₂/CuO/Au heterojunction solar cell.

Thesis project

TiO₂/CuO heterojunction solar cells

Grew TiO₂ films on glass, quartz and Si by pulsed laser deposition, built FTO/TiO₂/CuO/Au devices, characterized them with XRD, SEM-EDS, Raman and UV-Vis, and modeled defects and efficiency in SCAPS-1D.

Thin films

Oxide films and nanomaterials

PLD-grown ZnO films; Al-doped ZnO by a hydrothermal route; sol-gel iron oxide nanoparticles with nitrogen doping to control magnetic properties.

Ferroelectrics & magnetism

Doped BaTiO₃ and LiNbO₃

Pulsed laser annealing and laser-assisted CVD of doped BaTiO₃ and LiNbO₃ films, and the effect of annealing on their structure and magnetism, measured by SQUID magnetometry.

Computation

DFT and molecular dynamics

Structural, electronic and magnetic properties with Quantum ESPRESSO; bulk modulus, thermal expansion and diffusion with LAMMPS; run on Google Cloud and nanoHUB.

Instruments & techniques

Radiation & reliability testing

  • Single-event burnout (SEB), leakage current (SELC) and gate rupture (SEGR)
  • Total ionizing dose (TID)
  • Stress-induced leakage current (SILC) and bias stress
  • Bias temperature instability (NBTI/PBTI)

Electrical characterization

  • IV, CV, pulsed IV and high-power testing
  • Transfer and output curves; gate and drain leakage
  • Breakdown voltage, threshold voltage, RDS(on) and safe operating area
  • IV vs. temperature in a cryostat; Hall effect and magneto-transport
  • Keysight/Agilent B1505A, Keithley 4200A-SCS and 2410; manual probe station

Materials characterization

  • XRD (Bruker D8) with rocking curves and Rietveld refinement in TOPAS

  • SEM-EDS (FEI Quanta 200F) and AFM
  • SQUID magnetometry (Quantum Design MPMS)
  • UV-Vis spectroscopy

  • High-vacuum systems and chamber maintenance
